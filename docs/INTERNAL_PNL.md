## Transaction coverage and exclusions

- 200 physical source rows were preserved.
- Five exact duplicate rows were linked to canonical records and excluded.
- All 195 canonical transactions received classifications.
- 180 approved canonical transactions appear in the P&L.
- Twelve transfer-side transactions, one owner contribution, one owner
  distribution, and one fixed-asset purchase are balance-sheet activity and
  do not appear in the P&L.
- The application exposes the transaction drilldown behind every account line
  through the P&L interface and API.

The report builder recalculates account totals from the transaction drilldown,
then validates revenue, COGS, gross profit, operating expenses, and net profit
using `Decimal` values. The dollar amounts above are evidence captured from
that generated report set; they are not inputs to the calculation service.
